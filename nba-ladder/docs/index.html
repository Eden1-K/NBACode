How the model works

Show method
  • Team ratings. Each team has a net rating (points better than average). It starts from the preseason sportsbook win total and is updated after every game with a Bayesian fit on game margins (home-court adjusted, blowouts capped at 20, older games slowly weighted down). The preseason prior is worth ~16 games, so by mid-season results dominate.
  • Rest & travel. Second night of a back-to-back, 3 games in 4 nights, 2+ days off, time zones crossed and long road trips each shift the expected margin; the sizes were fitted on past seasons (). Past margins are rest-corrected before rating teams.
  • Tuning. Every setting (how much the preseason line is trusted, how fast old games fade, blowout cap, home court, rest, the probability spread and the uncertainty in the ranges) was chosen on 2023-24 and 2024-25, then checked on 2025-26, which was never used for tuning. Settings are only adopted if they beat the defaults on that held-out season.
  • Player availability. Each player's value comes from his box-score Game Score per 36 minutes (blended with last season early on) and his minutes. Players on the injury report reduce the team's rating for the games they're expected to miss (Out = full, Doubtful 80%, Questionable 50%, Day-to-day 35%; questionable players randomly sit or play in each simulation). Several absences have diminishing returns (each extra one counts 80% of the last, capped at 8 pts) because teammates absorb the minutes. Effects are scaled by how much of the season they've played, since the rating already reflects games they missed.
  • Simulation. The remaining schedule is played out 10,000 times. In each run, every team's true strength is drawn from its rating uncertainty plus an allowance for future injuries/trades, so ranges are wide early and narrow as the season goes on. Single-game spread (sigma, now 12.0 pts) and home court (now 1.5 pts) are re-estimated from this season's results.
  • Ladder. Seeds 1–15 per conference using NBA-style tiebreakers (record against the other tied teams, then conference record), then the play-in (7v8, 9v10, loser vs winner), then every playoff series best-of-7 game by game with 2-2-1-1-1 home court, for round-by-round and title odds.
  • Scores. The predicted margin comes from the ratings (so the predicted winner always matches the favourite). The predicted total comes from a second fit of how many combined points each team's games produce, starting from a 230-point league average. Score = (total ± margin) ÷ 2.
  • Confidence. Per-team 80% win ranges; ladder confidence measured inside the simulations; and an honest out-of-sample track record on games already played.
  • Track records. Walk-forward: every past game predicted using only earlier results (rest included; old injury reports aren't archived). Live: the exact prediction made before tip-off, frozen and scored afterwards. Both are compared with the betting market when ESPN publishes lines.
  • Data: ESPN public scoreboard, injury report and player stats. Updated daily by GitHub Actions.
